# Visual Direction — V2

## Overall Style

The visual language should feel:

- premium
- modern
- intimate
- slightly mysterious
- playful
- cinematic

It should not look like a traditional birthday template.

## Emotional Temperature

The visual system should move from cool/minimal to warm/immersive.

Do not make the entire website pink, pastel, or obviously "birthday themed" unless that genuinely matches N.

## Color

Recommended structure:

- Background
- Primary text
- Secondary text
- Accent
- Glow
- Soft warm highlight

Use a restrained palette.

The People Around You section may gradually introduce warmer light.

## Typography

Maximum two main typefaces.

### Display

Expressive typography for:

- chapter titles
- major statements
- final message

### Interface

Clean sans-serif for:

- names
- roles
- metadata
- controls

## Visual Metaphor

Use:

- dots
- lines
- light
- orbit
- windows
- signals
- connections

Avoid visual metaphors related to:

- wounds
- medical treatment
- broken faces
- masks
- reconstruction
- before/after imagery

The visual language should communicate connection, not damage.

## Central Node

Do not use an old portrait of N as the central visual.

Preferred options:

1. N's initial
2. her name
3. an abstract symbol
4. a designed avatar that does not attempt to recreate her face

This avoids making the face the subject of comparison.

## People Nodes

Each node can contain:

- avatar of contributor
- initials
- small identity marker

The contributor's presence matters more than their photographic relationship to N.

## Motion

Motion should feel:

- deliberate
- soft
- elegant
- surprising

Avoid aggressive glitch effects after the intro.

The visual language should increasingly feel calm and warm.
