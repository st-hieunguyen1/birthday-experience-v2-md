# Background Music Suggestions — V2

## Goal

Keep the emotional tone warm, calm, and present.

Avoid epic trailer energy, heavy sadness, or dramatic tension.

## Suggested BGM by Chapter

01–02 (Beginning + Character):

- soft ambient piano
- light lo-fi without heavy drums
- minimal synth pads

03–04 (Little Things + Stories):

- gentle indie instrumental
- warm acoustic texture
- subtle beat for movement

05 (People Around You):

- calm hopeful ambient
- intimate piano + light strings
- slow pulse, low intensity

06–07 (Everyone Is Here + Happy Birthday):

- uplifting but restrained cinematic ambient
- warm chord progression
- no dramatic drops

## Royalty-safe Sources

- YouTube Audio Library
- Pixabay Music
- Free Music Archive
- Incompetech
- Artlist / Epidemic Sound (paid)

Always verify license terms and attribution requirements before publish.

## Technical Notes

- Do not autoplay with sound on first load.
- Provide visible play/pause control.
- Target around -16 LUFS to -14 LUFS for consistent playback.
- Use one base loop + optional soft layer for finale.
- Fade transitions (300ms–1200ms) between chapter changes.

## Track Shortlist Starter Keywords

- "warm ambient piano"
- "hopeful minimal instrumental"
- "soft cinematic ambient"
- "gentle lo-fi no vocal"
- "intimate emotional underscore"
