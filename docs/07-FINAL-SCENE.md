# Final Scene — V2

## Purpose

Create the strongest emotional moment without using N's appearance as the emotional anchor.

## Initial State

After the final node closes:

The constellation remains visible.

Everything becomes quiet.

Then:

```text
EVERYONE'S HERE.
```

Pause.

## Transformation

The nodes slowly begin orbiting around the center.

The central symbol remains calm.

Connection lines become subtle.

The camera gradually pulls back.

The constellation begins to feel like a small universe.

## Final Wishes

Every contributor supplies one short final wish.

Examples:

- Hạnh phúc nhé!
- Luôn tươi cười nhé!
- Bình an nhé!
- Luôn vui nhé!
- Mãi rực rỡ nhé!
- Chúc chị thật nhiều niềm vui!
- Luôn được yêu thương nhé!
- Cứ là chính mình nhé!
- Mọi điều tốt đẹp sẽ đến nhé!

Avoid forcing every wish to mention recovery or strength.

## Visual Composition

The central symbol remains readable.

Wishes move around it.

They should create density without covering the center.

The final scene should feel like:

> a field of affection

rather than:

> a wall of text.

## Loop

The wishes continuously flow from below and travel around the central composition.

The loop should be seamless.

There should be no obvious restart.

## Final Message

Preferred:

```text
Happy Birthday, N.
```

Possible second line:

```text
Look around you.
```

Alternative:

```text
You are surrounded by people
who are happy to have you in their lives.
```

Use only one supporting message.

Do not over-explain.

## Important

Do not end with:

- "Get well soon."
- "Hope you return to your old self."
- "You will be beautiful again."
- "Everything will be exactly as before."

The ending should affirm presence, connection, and affection.
