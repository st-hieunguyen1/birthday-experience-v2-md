# Story Structure — V2

## Overview

Seven chapters:

01 — The Beginning
02 — Character
03 — Little Things
04 — Stories
05 — People Around You
06 — Everyone Is Here
07 — Happy Birthday

---

# CHAPTER 01 — THE BEGINNING

## Purpose

Create curiosity without immediately explaining the birthday or the emotional theme.

Possible opening:

```text
INITIALIZING...
Loading something special...
Loading memories...
Loading people...
Loading stories...
```

Progress reaches 99%.

Pause.

```text
ONE MORE THING...
```

Then:

```text
ARE YOU READY?
```

The interaction should feel playful rather than ominous.

---

# CHAPTER 02 — CHARACTER

## Purpose

Introduce N through qualities that have nothing to do with appearance.

Possible attributes:

- Creativity
- Leadership
- Empathy
- Patience
- Humor
- Design Sense
- People Skills
- Energy
- Curiosity

Alternative presentation:

## WHAT PEOPLE KNOW YOU FOR

> The one who notices.

> The one who makes things beautiful.

> The one who takes care of people.

> The one who makes everyone laugh.

> The one who somehow gets things done.

Avoid beauty or appearance ratings.

---

# CHAPTER 03 — LITTLE THINGS

## Purpose

Show that meaningful relationships are built from ordinary moments.

Instead of a conventional photo gallery, use small fragments:

- a sentence
- a message
- a funny moment
- a work memory
- a shared meal
- a tiny inside joke
- a short video
- an object associated with a memory

The point is not "look how beautiful the past was."

The point is:

> "Look at all the little things that became memories."

---

# CHAPTER 04 — STORIES

Transition from individual memories to people.

Suggested narrative:

```text
A story is never only about one person.
```

Pause.

```text
Every chapter has people in it.
```

Pause.

```text
Some stay for a moment.
Some stay much longer.
```

Then:

```text
And some...
```

Fade.

```text
are still here.
```

Transition into:

# PEOPLE AROUND YOU

---

# CHAPTER 05 — PEOPLE AROUND YOU

This is the emotional centerpiece.

N becomes the central node.

People close to her appear around her.

The number can be small or large.

The system should support both.

Each person is a node containing a curated personal contribution.

---

# CHAPTER 06 — EVERYONE IS HERE

When all required nodes have been opened:

The constellation becomes still.

Then:

```text
EVERYONE'S HERE.
```

The nodes begin to move.

They orbit around N.

The visual field gradually becomes warmer.

The user realizes that the entire experience was leading toward this moment.

---

# CHAPTER 07 — HAPPY BIRTHDAY

The final scene contains:

- N as a neutral central symbol / initial / abstract representation
- people orbiting around her
- final wishes flowing around the center
- subtle continuous motion

Final message:

```text
Happy Birthday, N.
```

Optional supporting line:

```text
Look around you.
```

or:

```text
You are surrounded by people who are happy to have you in their lives.
```
