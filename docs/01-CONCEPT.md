# Concept & Creative Direction — V2

## Working Concept

### THE WORLD AROUND N

Alternative titles:

- People Around You
- The World Around N
- You Are Surrounded
- All Around You
- A Little World Around N
- The People Who Are Here

The title should not be revealed too early.

## Central Thought

The original concept focused on N as a "main character".

The revised concept keeps the game-like experience but changes what the player is ultimately discovering.

The user begins by thinking:

> "This is a birthday game."

They gradually discover:

> "This game is about me."

Then:

> "Wait. These are all people I know."

And finally:

> "There are so many people around me."

The emotional destination is not appearance.

It is belonging.

## Creative Principle

Do not attempt to make N forget what happened.

Do not attempt to convince N that everything is fine.

Do not force optimism.

Instead, create a space where she can simply receive affection.

## What We Avoid

Avoid:

- before/after comparisons
- old photos as the emotional centerpiece
- "you are still beautiful" messaging
- "get well soon" as the dominant message
- "be strong" as an obligation
- language implying that her value depends on appearance
- medical references
- dramatic references to the accident
- forced inspirational quotes
- excessive sadness

## What We Emphasize

Emphasize:

- personality
- memories
- friendship
- work
- creativity
- leadership
- humor
- kindness
- small moments
- people who remember her
- people who chose to show up
- ordinary affection

## Emotional Rule

The website should feel like people gently gathering around N.

Not like people gathering around a problem.

## Design Escalation

### Beginning

Minimal, mysterious, quiet.

### Middle

Playful, interactive, curious.

### People Around You

Warm, personal, intimate.

### Finale

Cinematic, expansive, reassuring.

The emotional intensity should rise without becoming melodramatic.
