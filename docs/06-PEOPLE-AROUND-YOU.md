# People Around You — V2

## Purpose

This is the emotional heart of the website.

The section answers one question:

> **Who is around N?**

Not physically at this exact moment.

But in the larger sense:

> Who remembers her?
> Who cares about her?
> Who has shared a part of her life?
> Who chose to send something for her birthday?

## Visual Metaphor

N is represented by a central symbol.

Around her are people.

Each person becomes a small world.

The constellation represents connection.

## Central Node

The central node should not depend on N's face.

Recommended:

```text
N
```

or:

```text
[N'S NAME]
```

or a custom abstract symbol.

The central element should be visually calm.

## Contributor Node

Each node may contain:

- avatar
- name
- role / relationship if useful
- subtle status

Do not require a photo with N.

A contributor's own photo or avatar is enough.

## Memory Window

Opening a node should feel like opening a small personal window.

Possible structure:

```text
[Avatar]

NAME

RELATIONSHIP

"Personal message..."

[Photo / Video]

"Final wish"
```

## Content Types

Support:

### Text

A personal message.

### Photo

A meaningful memory.

### Video

A short message.

### Multiple photos

A small curated set.

### Voice

Optional if technically convenient.

## Content Philosophy

Ask contributors:

> "What do you remember when you think about N?"

rather than:

> "Send your best photo with N."

This encourages memories that are about the person rather than appearance.

## Sensitive Content Rules

Do not publish:

- medical details
- hospital images
- accident images
- comments about scars
- comments comparing current and previous appearance
- unsolicited statements about recovery
- "you will be beautiful again" type messages
- anything that makes appearance the measure of wellbeing

Unless N herself has explicitly asked for such content, it does not belong in the birthday experience.

## Discovery

After opening a node:

```text
VIEWED
```

The node remains part of the constellation.

The user should be able to return to it.

## Completion

After all nodes are viewed:

```text
EVERYONE'S HERE.
```

The entire constellation becomes active.

## Emotional Interpretation

The section should make N feel:

> "There are people here."

Not:

> "People are worried about me."

That distinction is critical.
